Is This You?

Who this is built for.

This program works extremely well for the right agent and not at all for the wrong one. We would rather both of us know before the call.

You may be ready if you…

  • Hold an active life and health insurance license
  • Want a defined specialty instead of another generic market
  • Will complete the curriculum and learn the presentation
  • Are ready to call warm prospects and set appointments
  • Operate with discipline, disclosure, and follow-through

This is not for someone who…

  • Is looking for a salary, job, or guaranteed income
  • Wants passive leads without making calls
  • Is not currently licensed in a resident state
  • Expects software or marketing to sell for them
  • Is unwilling to master a consultative presentation
