Common Questions

What partners ask first.

Q

Do you take a cut of my commissions?

No. The launch fee and the monthly cover the platform. What you write is yours.

Q

Do I have to be licensed already?

You need an active life and health license — or a firm plan to get one — before your first lead drops.

Q

Who owns the leads and the website?

The leads are yours and are never shared or resold. The site is built and hosted on our platform under your name and domain, for as long as you're an active partner.

Q

How fast can I actually be live?

14 days from your onboarding call, assuming you complete the training day and get us your photo, bio, and domain preference on schedule.
