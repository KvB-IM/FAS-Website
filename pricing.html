Common Questions

What partners ask first.

Q

Do you take a cut of my commissions?

No. The launch investment and the monthly cover the program and platform. What you write is yours.

Q

Do I have to be licensed already?

You need an active life and health license — or a firm plan to get one — before your first lead drops.

Q

Who owns the leads and the website?

The leads are yours and are never shared or resold. The site is built and hosted on our platform under your name and domain, for as long as you're an active partner.

Q

When do I receive leads?

Lead delivery follows contracting, training, presentation readiness, and website launch — your leads do not arrive before you are prepared to work them. Package A includes an initial 50 warm leads; it does not promise protected territories or guaranteed daily lead volume.
